## Coordinator's Preliminary Position and Objections to the Own Team

1. First the lead team completes and accepts the 11 blockers, then the current snapshot. X has no authority to change their statuses. S6-D01/D02 and C1/C2/C3 do not become repeat backlog; ARIA tabs S6-A01 were already changed by C4 and removed by X1.
2. Of the small UI items, X1-I02 has direct logical evidence; X1-I01 first requires reproduction in the DOM. For I03/I04, usefulness should be confirmed with stock LuCI and accessibility, not by treating object stubs as a complete check. I05 depends on the final C1 CLI.
3. X2-01 has grounds for limited research, but a cache does not shrink the final JSON/Xray and may increase live memory. The wording about repeated protobuf parsing needs verification: repeated expansion does not necessarily mean reparsing the entire DB. RSS savings must not be claimed without a new pair of measurements. Do not include streaming automatically together with the cache.
4. X2-04 has the most concrete new evidence of the wait structure. However, the contract does not promise a shorter failover time; the 40 ms are set by the fixture. Verification of the probe queue (a group's readiness does not equal the ability to launch its candidates earlier), generation semantics, and real benefit is needed before a complex scheduler change.
5. X2-02/03/05 are conditional optimizations. Quadratic complexity must not be presented as the main bottleneck, 16×4 MiB as peak RSS, or callbacks as the measured fork/API cost. Deadlines and restoring the selection after respawn matter more than savings that have no measurements yet.
6. X2-08 is so far only an investigation of the guarantee gap. A new small reproduction on the final C1 and selection of a minimal solution are required. A high priority for a specific journal does not automatically follow from the historical F-S2-01. A new internal file may carry costs and backup limitations; the journal must not become a new mandatory user artifact. Terra is asked to challenge the priority, cost, and the design choice itself.
7. X2-06 should be limited to the current diagnostics and safe brief states. X2-07 has no proven flash benefit; repeated manual updates may be rare. X2-09 may be partially completed by the lead team by the end of closure; propose only the confirmed remainder.
